## 📌 Project Overview
Designed and developed an interactive Car Sales Analytics Dashboard 
in Excel to analyze vehicle pricing, sales distribution, and market 
trends across Nigerian states and cities.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
The used car market generates large volumes of transactional data 
that is difficult to interpret without structured analysis. This 
project transforms raw car listing data into a clear, interactive 
dashboard that supports pricing and market decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze used car sales data and uncover insights around pricing 
patterns, top-selling models, geographical distribution, and vehicle 
conditions across Nigeria.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

---

## 🔧 Data Preparation & Cleaning
- Cleaned raw car listings dataset in Excel
- Handled missing values across key columns
- Standardized data types for numerical and categorical fields
- Structured data model to support pivot table analysis

---

## 📐 Data Modeling & Analysis
- Built Pivot Tables to summarize sales by model, state, and condition
- Calculated Average Selling Price and Average Horsepower
- Analyzed Top 10 Car Models by count
- Mapped geographical distribution across Nigerian states

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **KPI Summary** — Average price and horsepower metrics
- **Top 10 Car Models** — ranked by volume
- **State Distribution** — sales count by Nigerian state
- **Vehicle Condition Analysis** — Foreign Used vs Nigerian Used
- **Interactive Slicers** — dynamic filtering by make, state, and year
- **Fuel Type & Transmission breakdown**

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Toyota and Honda are among the highest volume car brands
- Lagos and FCT account for the majority of listings
- Foreign Used vehicles command significantly higher prices
- Automatic transmission dominates the used car market
- Average selling price across all listings is ₦4,986,147

---

## 💼 Business Impact
The dashboard enables dealers and buyers to quickly compare pricing, 
identify high-demand models, and understand regional market trends 
to make smarter buying and selling decisions.
